01 / Course design

A shared foundation.
New conversations each semester.

The common syllabus combines lectures, guest presentations, and interactive sessions. Its goals emphasize familiarity with robotics research, direct interaction with researchers, and connections across machine learning, HRI, soft robotics, and medical robotics.

01

Make research approachable

Expert presentations introduce research problems, methods, and challenges across robotics. The seminar format creates space to explore unfamiliar ideas through conversation.

02

Make discussion meaningful

The syllabus assesses attendance and participation, including relevant questions and thoughtful contributions to seminar discussions.

04 / Course overview

Learning through participation.

The course emphasizes familiarity with current robotics research, interaction with researchers and professionals, and connections between robotics and other fields. Assessment centers on attendance and active participation rather than traditional exams or projects.

One common syllabus provides the foundation; guest speakers and practical sessions vary by semester.
